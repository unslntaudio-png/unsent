About

The song comes first.

Ros-e is Tom Rosenberg, a music producer in Tel Aviv and a graduate of Rimon School of Music.

His sound comes out of RnB and electronic music: programmed beats next to real players, warm low end, and room for the vocal to lead. He works with artists from the first voice memo to the finished record.

He also runs UNSLNT., a studio that writes original music for TV and advertising.

Building the track around the song: beats, sounds, live instruments and the direction of the record.

Taking a demo or a guitar-and-voice idea and giving it shape, dynamics and a structure that holds attention.

Co-writing music, toplines and hooks with the artist, in Hebrew or English.

Recording sessions, comping, harmonies and the small details that make a vocal sit right.
