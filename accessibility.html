Accessibility statement

Ros-e wants everyone to be able to use this website. We work to make it readable, navigable and usable for people with a wide range of abilities.

What we have done

  • An accessibility button (bottom corner of every page) with text size, contrast, link highlighting, readable font, animation stop options. Your choices are saved on your device.
  • Keyboard navigation with visible focus, a "skip to content" link and labelled controls.
  • Descriptive labels for video play buttons and images, and a respect for the "reduce motion" system setting.
  • A responsive layout that works on phones, tablets and desktops.

Known limitations

Videos are hosted by YouTube and Spotify and are played by their own players, so their captions and controls depend on those services. Some videos may not have captions. We are working to improve this.
